How to read the map

Start here

  1. Each coloured spoke is a subject area. Click a subject to unfold its instruments, or use Expand all.
  2. Click any instrument to open its dossier: concepts, obligations, deadlines, breach and links.
  3. When one is selected, its connections light up across the map.
  4. An amber marks entries with unverified items.
